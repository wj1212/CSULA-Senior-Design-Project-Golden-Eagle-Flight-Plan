# General Education Requirements for CS Majors at Cal State LA

## Overview
- **Total Degree Units**: 120 units
- **Units in Major**: 93 units
- **GE Units Required**: 43 units (21 of which are satisfied by major courses)

## GE Areas Satisfied by Major Courses

### GE 1B - Critical Thinking
**WAIVED for CS majors** - This area is satisfied by the combination of multiple CS courses. It will appear as 'waived' in your degree audit.

### GE Area 2 - Math/Quantitative Reasoning
**MATH 2110** - Calculus I (4 units)
- Required for the major AND satisfies GE Area 2

### GE Area 3B - Humanities  
**CS 3801** - Societal and Ethical Issues in Computing (3 units)
- Required for the major AND satisfies GE Area 3B

### GE Area 5A & 5C - Physical Science + Lab
**PHYS 2100** - General Physics I: Mechanics (4 units)
- Required for the major AND satisfies both GE Area 5A (Physical Science) and Area 5C (Lab)

### Upper Division GE Area 2 or 5 - Math/Science
**CS 3112** - Analysis of Algorithms (3 units) AND
**CS 3186** - Introduction to Automata Theory (3 units)
- These two courses TOGETHER satisfy UD GE Area 2 or 5

### Upper Division GE Area 3 & 4 - Arts/Humanities & Social Sciences
**CS 4961** - Software Design Laboratory I (3 units) AND
**CS 4962** - Software Design Laboratory II (3 units)
- These two courses TOGETHER satisfy BOTH UD GE Area 3 (Arts/Humanities) AND Area 4 (Social Sciences)
- Also marked as (wi) Writing Intensive and (cl) Capstone/Learning

## Important Notes
1. Some courses satisfy multiple requirements (dual-counted)
2. GE 1B is waived - no need to look for a specific course for this requirement
3. Several upper division courses work in pairs to satisfy GE requirements
4. Prerequisites in the correct order
